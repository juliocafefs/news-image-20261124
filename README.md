# The World in One Day — 24 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun competition
[news-image-20261124](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124):
"Sum up 24 November's world news in one image."

The entry image is a classic broadsheet front page for **Tuesday
24 November 2026**, drawn in type alone — ivory paper, black ink,
hairline rules, no photographs:

- **Lead — Hormuz on a knife-edge**: Iran says three oil tankers
  struck naval mines and burned in the Strait of Hormuz; Tehran
  rules out talks with Washington until its seven conditions are
  met, among them an end to the US naval blockade of Iranian ports.
- **Ukraine** — a Russian glide-bomb attack on Zaporizhzhia killed
  two civilians and wounded 48, four of them children; Kyiv's naval
  drones struck a Russian warship docked at Benghazi, Libya.
- **The Hague** — fourteen countries affirmed the International
  Court of Justice's ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against
  the Rohingya; Britain added sanctions on senior military figures.
- **Markets** — Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO and
  the shares leapt 31%; the S&P 500 rose 4.73% to 7,237.17 and the
  Nasdaq 6.51% to 24,089.56.
- **In brief** — Oxfam calls Gaza a "toxic land"; China named the
  Shenzhou-24 crew, with Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali the first
  foreigner to visit Tiangong; a magnitude 5.6 earthquake struck
  Taplejung, Nepal; the UN Secretary-General urged a global AI-risk
  framework; North Korea ordered Switzerland's Pyongyang office
  closed; a Hanoi restaurant blast killed six.

Every story was reported on 24 November 2026 and checked against at
least two independent reports before it was drawn.

## How it works

`build_image.py` draws the front page with Pillow and writes
`entry.png` (1024×1024). `main.ts` (Node 22) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`). No
network access is used at run time.
